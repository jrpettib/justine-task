Informed Consent

You are being invited to participate in a research study conducted as part of an undergraduate research course. The study examines how ADHD symptoms might impact choices related to effort and delay. The study is designed by Justine Klein, who is pursuing a bachelor's degree from Michigan Technological University and supervised by Dr. Jeffrey Pettibone PhD, from the Department of Psychology and Human Factors at Michigan Technological University. Your participation in this study is entirely voluntary, and you are free at any time to opt out/withdraw from the study by closing your browser window. The study should take around 30 minutes.

Participation Requirements

You must be 18 years or older to participate. You will be asked to complete 48 cognitive trials and then fill out a brief 20 item questionnaire at the end. Participation will require a phone, or tablet with a touchscreen or a laptop with a mouse, and a stable internet connection.

Potential Risks

Participation in the study does not involve any risk to you beyond that of everyday life.

Potential Benefits

The potential benefit for this study is to contribute to the scientific literature on the understanding of ADHD. There is no direct benefit to you.

Compensation

Participation in this study will not provide any direct reward or compensation to you.

Confidentiality

No personally identifiable information will be collected. For surveys, participants' email will not be recorded. Only the Principal investigators will have access to the data collected.

Practice Phase

You will now complete two practice trials to familiarize yourself with the task. The goal is to detect the letter ’T’ among a set of quickly changing letter streams. The first practice trial will be the easy, low effort version with 2 streams of letters. The second practice trial will be the difficult, high effort version with 6 streams of letters. The letter ’T’ will appear 3-5 times. Try to detect all of them without missing any (Misses) or responding when one isn’t there (False Alarms).

Press the Spacebar or Tap the Screen every time you see the letter 'T'.

Main Task

Now that you have completed the practice trials, we will begin the main phase of the study.

In this phase, every block will begin with a choice, each associated with a different reward amount.

On some trials, you will choose between Low Difficulty (2 letters presented simultaneously) or High Difficulty (6 letters presented simultaneously). On other trials, you will choose between Short Delay (2 seconds until you see whether you were rewarded) and Long Delay (20 seconds until you see whether you were rewarded).

Try to detect as many ’T’s as possible while minimizing Misses and False Alarms. Blocks with more than two misses or more than three False Alarms will not be awarded points.

Your goal is to maximize the number of points you accumulate over 48 blocks.

Your current total points are shown on the upper right corner of your screen.

A brief demographic survey will follow regarding ADHD symptoms.
